Common fixes

  • The game looks out of date: close it completely and open it again. New versions download in the background and install on the next launch.
  • No sound: tap the screen once (phones block sound until the first tap), then check the 🔊 and 🎵 buttons and the ⚙ settings.
  • Lost progress: progress is saved only on this device. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling resets it.
