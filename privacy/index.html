SKYJINN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Summary

SKYJINN does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores, and where

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device, in the browser storage the game runs in (localStorage and the offline cache):

  • campaign stars and unlocked stages;
  • gems and Forge upgrades;
  • daily challenge status;
  • settings such as music on or off and the chosen difficulty.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's or site's data, deletes it.

Network use

The game downloads its own files (code, art, music) from our web host so it can run and work offline. Like any web server, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Children

SKYJINN does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
